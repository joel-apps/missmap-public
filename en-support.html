Missmap

Golf course-management training: a daily round of nine holes of where-to-aim decisions, with a heat map of what each target costs a golfer like you.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or an answer you think is wrong? On any answer in the app, tap Report an error (it fills in which decision you were looking at), or email joelhcode@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Missmap and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal or trial end date.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Your rounds, streak and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them; your purchase can always be restored.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No to play: every hole, map and answer is built into the app. A connection is used for purchases and anonymous usage statistics. On the free plan, new daily holes may wait until you reconnect if you have been offline for more than a day or two.

How do I change my golfer profile, yards or metres, language or reminders?

Open Settings in the app (the gear at the top of the main screen). Your usual score, your usual miss, distances, language, the daily reminder and vibration each have their own setting.

Where do the answers come from?

From our own simulation of thousands of shots per target, using golfer profiles based on published research on amateur shot data. They are model estimates for a typical golfer at each level. In the app, Settings → How we calculate this lists the sources and simplifications.
