Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what the Missmap iPhone app does with your data. Missmap is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”). Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. Your rounds and settings stay on your iPhone. We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app.

Data on your device

Your answers, rounds, streak, golfer profile and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Apart from your first-run answers (see Anonymous usage statistics) and anything you choose to email us, they are not sent to us, and the app does not sync them to any server; your iPhone’s own iCloud Backup may include them.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used: when the app is installed, updated, opened or closed, when the first-run questions start and finish, your answers to them (your usual score band and your usual miss), when the purchase screen is shown and from where, which plan you choose and whether the purchase completes, and when you tap Restore purchases. Events include the app version, device model, iOS version, screen size, language and time zone. They are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email, Apple ID or any other identity. PostHog stores these events in the United States. It uses your IP address only to estimate an approximate location (country and city), then discards it; we configure it not to keep IP addresses. We use this data only to improve the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier, plus the app version, device model, iOS version, language and your IP address as part of the connection.

Emails to us

If you email us (for example with Report an error on an answer), we receive your email address and what your message contains, including the decision the app fills in for you (the golfer profile, hole and shot). You see all of it before sending. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the content, and keep it only as long as needed for that.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

What we don’t do

We don’t ask for your name, email or any account. We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers. We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We don’t sell or share your data, except with the service providers named above for the purposes described. These providers process the data only on our behalf and are required to protect it at least as well as this policy describes.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept for as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases, or as the law requires; email us to ask for them to be deleted. Because usage statistics and purchase records are anonymous, we usually cannot link them to you, but you can email us with any request about your data and we will help as far as we can.

Children

Missmap is suitable for all ages. It has no accounts and does not ask for names, emails or other contact details. The anonymous usage statistics described above are collected the same way for every user.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
